This Annex forms part of and is incorporated by reference into the following underlying agreements between the same parties (each an "Underlying Agreement"):

- Maintenance and Support Agreement(s).

1. Subject Matter and Duration
- Subject matter: The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller for the purposes of providing the services under the Underlying Agreement(s), including API operation and technical support/maintenance as applicable.
- Duration: This DPA applies for the term of the relevant Underlying Agreement and ends upon deletion/return of personal data in accordance with Section 10.

2. Nature and Purpose of Processing
- Nature: Collection, receipt, storage, transmission, structuring, retrieval, and other processing necessary to provide the API and/or perform support/maintenance.
- Purpose: Provision of the API service, metering/usage reporting and billing, and handling support/maintenance requests.

3. Categories of Data Subjects and Personal Data
- Data subjects: Controller’s personnel and end users (as applicable).
- Categories of personal data (as applicable): Business contact data (name, email, phone), technical identifiers (IP address, token/ID, request metadata), usage/log data, and other personal data submitted by Controller via the services. Special categories of data per Art. 9(1) GDPR are not intended to be processed.

4. Roles and Instructions
- Roles: Controller is the controller; Processor is the processor (Art. 4 GDPR).
- Instructions: Processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from Controller, including with regard to transfers to a third country, unless required by Union or Member State law. Processor shall promptly inform Controller if an instruction infringes GDPR or other data protection provisions.

5. Confidentiality
- Processor ensures that persons authorised to process personal data are bound by confidentiality and receive appropriate data protection training.

6. Security of Processing (Art. 32 GDPR)
- Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (TOMs) to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk. The current TOMs include, as appropriate:
  - Access control: role-based access, least privilege, unique accounts, MFA for administrative access.
  - Data protection: encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest where supported; data minimisation and segregation; secure key management.
  - Application security: change management, code review for changes, dependency management, and vulnerability remediation.
  - Operational security: logging and monitoring, backup and recovery for critical systems, incident response procedures.
  - Physical security: data centres operated by reputable providers with industry-standard controls.
  - Business continuity: reasonable measures to restore availability and access to personal data in a timely manner following an incident.
- Processor may update TOMs provided the overall security level is not materially decreased.

7. Subprocessors (Art. 28(2)–(4) GDPR)
- Authorisation: Controller grants a general authorisation for Processor to engage subprocessors for the services.
- List and notification: Upon request, Processor will provide a current list of subprocessors. Processor will notify Controller of intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of subprocessors, giving Controller the opportunity to object on reasonable grounds within 15 days of notice. If Controller reasonably objects and the parties cannot resolve within a reasonable period, Controller may terminate the affected services as its sole remedy.
- Flow-down: Processor shall impose data protection obligations on subprocessors equivalent to this DPA and remain responsible for their performance.

8. International Transfers
- Processor will process personal data within the EU/EEA where practicable. For transfers to third countries lacking an adequacy decision, Processor shall ensure appropriate safeguards under Chapter V GDPR, including execution of the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) with supplementary measures where necessary. Where subprocessors are involved, Processor will ensure appropriate transfer mechanisms are in place.

9. Assistance to Controller
- Data subject rights: Taking into account the nature of processing, Processor shall assist Controller by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as possible, for the fulfilment of Controller’s obligations to respond to requests under Chapter III GDPR.
- Security and DPIA: Processor shall assist Controller in ensuring compliance with Articles 32 to 36 GDPR, including security, personal data breach notification, data protection impact assessments, and prior consultations, taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to Processor.

10. Personal Data Breach
- Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting personal data processed for Controller and provide information reasonably required for Controller to meet its notification obligations under Articles 33 and 34 GDPR, as such information becomes available.

11. Return and Deletion of Data
- Upon termination of the relevant Underlying Agreement, and after any agreed export period, Processor shall, at Controller’s choice, delete or return personal data and delete existing copies, unless Union or Member State law requires storage of the personal data. Deletion will be performed from active systems within a reasonable period consistent with standard backup cycles.

12. Audits and Information
- Upon reasonable prior notice (at least 30 days) and no more than once per 12 months, Controller may audit Processor’s compliance with this DPA. Audits shall, where feasible, be conducted remotely and limited to information reasonably necessary to verify compliance, without disrupting Processor’s operations or compromising Processor’s or third parties’ confidentiality or security. Controller may rely on third-party audit reports or certifications made available by Processor. On-site audits (if strictly necessary) shall be conducted during normal business hours, under confidentiality, and at Controller’s cost unless a material breach is found.

13. Records of Processing and Cooperation
- Processor shall maintain records of processing activities as required by Article 30(2) GDPR and make them available to supervisory authorities upon request. Processor shall cooperate with competent supervisory authorities upon request in relation to the processing performed under this DPA.

14. Controller Responsibilities
- Controller is responsible for the lawfulness of the processing, obtaining necessary consents or other legal bases, providing required notices to data subjects, and for the accuracy, quality, and legality of personal data provided to Processor.
