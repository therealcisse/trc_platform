## 2. Delivery and Acceptance

2.1 The Developer will deliver the Software as an obfuscated JavaScript build (and associated runtime assets) suitable for execution, without un-obfuscated source code or the development toolchain. No documentation is included unless expressly agreed in writing.
2.2 The Customer shall test the Software within 14 days of delivery. If the Software materially conforms to the agreed specifications, the Customer shall declare acceptance in writing. If no written objection is made within this period, the Software shall be deemed accepted.

2.3 Secure Electronic Delivery (Download Link + Checksum)
(a) Packaging and Naming. The Software deliverable will be packaged as a single .zip archive with a clear versioned filename in the form productname_vX.Y.Z_YYYY-MM-DD.zip.
(b) Checksum. Before delivery, the Developer computes an SHA-256 checksum for the archive (the “Checksum”).
(c) Delivery Notice and Delivery Date. The Developer will send an email in Textform pursuant to Section 7.3/7.7 to the notice addresses, containing (i) a private, expiring download link valid for at least seven (7) calendar days (the “Link Validity Period”) and (ii) the Checksum (the “Delivery Notice”). The timestamp of sending the Delivery Notice is the “Delivery Date.”
(d) Customer Download and Verification. The Customer shall download the archive within the Link Validity Period and verify integrity by computing an SHA-256 hash of the downloaded file and comparing it to the Checksum. The Customer will acknowledge receipt and integrity by reply email; failure to acknowledge does not affect Delivery or the acceptance timeline.
(e) Access or Integrity Issues. If the Customer cannot access the download link or observes a checksum mismatch, the Customer shall notify the Developer without undue delay and no later than three (3) business days after the Delivery Date. The Developer will promptly re-issue a download link and Checksum. In case of a checksum mismatch confirmed by the Developer, the review/acceptance period in Section 2.2 restarts from the re-issue date.
(f) Evidence. The Delivery Notice and the Checksum constitute authoritative evidence of the content of the delivered file. The Developer may retain the Delivery Notice and related logs as proof of delivery.
(g) Confidentiality. The Customer shall treat the download link and the delivered package as Confidential Information and shall disclose the link only to personnel authorized to receive the Software under this Agreement.

2.4 Effect on Acceptance Period
For purposes of Section 2.2, “delivery” occurs on the Delivery Date. The fourteen (14) day review/acceptance period runs from the Delivery Date; if a checksum mismatch is confirmed and a re-issue occurs, the fourteen (14) day period restarts from the re-issue date.
