3. The data we process, purposes, and legal bases
We only process personal data where a legal basis under the GDPR applies. Below we describe the categories of data, purposes, and applicable legal bases (Art. 6(1) GDPR). In Germany, cookie/trackers also require consent under TTDSG §25 unless strictly necessary.

3.1 Account and authentication
- Data: Email address, password (stored as a hash), verification tokens, account status, timestamps.
- Purpose: Create and manage your account, authenticate access, secure your session, support you.
- Legal basis: Performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps (Art. 6(1)(b)); legitimate interests in security (Art. 6(1)(f)); legal obligations where applicable (Art. 6(1)(c)).

3.2 Usage and billing
- Data: Request and usage metadata, plan/period information, billing totals, invoices or receipts where applicable.
- Purpose: Provide the Services, track usage, calculate fees, issue invoices/receipts, handle customer support and disputes.
- Legal basis: Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); legal obligations for bookkeeping/tax (Art. 6(1)(c)); legitimate interests such as service performance and fraud detection (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3.3 API tokens and developer use
- Data: Token name, token prefix, status/revocation, last-used timestamps, related account ID.
- Purpose: Authenticate API calls, prevent abuse, secure the platform.
- Legal basis: Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); legitimate interests in security and anti-abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3.4 Content you submit (images, prompts, etc.)
- Data: Files and content you upload, derived outputs and processing metadata.
- Purpose: Provide core functionality (AI-based image analysis and results), support and debugging, fulfill your requests.
- Legal basis: Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); legitimate interests in improving reliability and support (Art. 6(1)(f)). Where we rely on your consent for optional uses, we will ask you first (Art. 6(1)(a)).
- Note: Please avoid uploading special categories of personal data (GDPR Art. 9) unless strictly necessary and lawful.

3.5 Log files and security telemetry
- Data: IP address, timestamps, user agent, request IDs, error traces, limited request context.
- Purpose: Ensure availability, security, fraud prevention, debugging and incident response.
- Legal basis: Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3.6 Cookies and similar technologies
- Strictly necessary cookies: Essential for the website and login to work; do not require consent (TTDSG §25(2)).
- Non-essential cookies (e.g., analytics/marketing): Used only with your prior opt-in consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), TTDSG §25(1)). You can withdraw consent at any time via the cookie settings.

3.7 Communications and support
- Data: Email address, message content, metadata.
- Purpose: Respond to your inquiries, provide customer support.
- Legal basis: Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) or legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3.8 Payments (if/when offered)
- Data: Limited billing data (e.g., plan, invoice records). Payment card data is typically processed directly by the payment provider.
- Purpose: Process payments, handle refunds or disputes, comply with tax and accounting law.
- Legal basis: Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).

4. Sources of data
We collect data directly from you (e.g., during registration, while using the Services) and indirectly from your device (e.g., logs, cookies). If third parties are involved (e.g., payment processors, analytics providers), they may share limited data back as necessary to perform the Services.

If personal data is transferred to countries outside the EU/EEA, we rely on approved transfer mechanisms such as:

You can contact us for details of the transfer mechanisms applicable to your data.

7. Retention
We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above, or as required by law:
- Account and contract data: For the duration of your account, and thereafter as required for claims handling and statutory retention.
- Billing and tax records: Typically up to 10 years under German commercial and tax law (e.g., HGB §257, AO §147).
- Security logs: Typically up to 90 days unless needed longer for incident handling.
- API tokens: For as long as tokens remain active; revoked tokens and minimal audit logs may be retained for security and compliance.
- Uploaded content: For as long as needed to provide the Services and as you direct; shorter if you delete it, subject to backups and legal retention.

8. Your rights
Under the GDPR, you have the following rights (subject to statutory conditions):
- Access (Art. 15)
- Rectification (Art. 16)
- Erasure (Art. 17)
- Restriction (Art. 18)
- Data portability (Art. 20)
- Objection (Art. 21)
- Withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3))

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77). In Hamburg, the authority is: Der Hamburgische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit — https://datenschutz-hamburg.de.

To exercise your rights, contact: amadou.cisse@proton.me.
