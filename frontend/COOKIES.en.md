# Cookies Policy — Image Solve Platform

Last updated: 23 September 2025

1. What cookies we use
We currently only use cookies that are strictly necessary for the website and the logged-in app to function. These include, for example:
- Session cookies (e.g., Django sessionid) to keep you signed in
- CSRF protection cookie (e.g., csrftoken) to secure form/API submissions

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies at this time.

2. Legal basis and consent (Germany/EU)
Under the German TTDSG §25 and the EU ePrivacy rules, strictly necessary cookies do not require consent. Because we only use essential cookies, no cookie banner is required right now. If we later introduce non-essential cookies (e.g., analytics, marketing), we will request your opt-in consent before setting them and update this policy accordingly.

3. Managing cookies
You can control cookies via your browser. Disabling strictly necessary cookies will impact sign-in and core functionalities.

4. Changes
We may update this Cookies Policy when our use of cookies changes. We will indicate the effective date above and seek consent for new non-essential cookies where required.
